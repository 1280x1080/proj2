# THE LAST BELL — series bible

Psychological horror / romance, 8 parts. Read before writing each part; update after it ships and when the comments decide.

## Premise
You stay after school with the girl you've liked for months to finish a science-fair project in **Room 2B**. A
lockdown alarm goes off, and something in the hallway starts knocking. It talks in **her voice** while she sits right
next to you. It knows things about you. And she's hiding something about "last time".

## Format (every part)
* 9:16, ~100 s, second-person present-tense narration; follow all of `STUDIO/STANDARDS.md` (emotion presets per line
  for HER and YOU, lip-sync for HER, caption style v2 with speaker labels, overlap check, no dead air).
* Voices (Qwen3-TTS, designed once, reused every part — files in `VIDEOS/VIDEO_009/voices/`): narrator (young woman,
  hushed) · **HER** (teen girl, soft) · **OUTSIDE** = HER voice clone heard through the door (`fx: door`) ·
  **INTERCOM** (principal, `fx: intercom`). Speaker labels: `HER`, `OUTSIDE · HER VOICE?`, `INTERCOM`.
* The thing outside is never shown clearly: a silhouette behind frosted glass, knocks, the handle, the light under the
  door, shadows on the floor.
* Look: warm afternoon classroom → emergency red from the alarm on (darken the room, never the faces).
* Hook = 3 s flash-forward (laughing → lights red → door + knock) + on-screen hook text.
* Choice screen: "WHAT DO YOU DO?" + A/B pills pinned to the objects with the risk under each;
  "COMMENT A OR B · YOUR DECISION DETERMINES PART N+1". The top comment decides.

## Rules of the thing (hinted, never stated outright)
1. It can borrow the voice of someone you care about.
2. It can't open a door unless someone answers it (the handle only turns after the voice said "you know I like you" — after it *learned* something).
3. Shadows: if it touches your shadow it "remembers" you (and takes your place?).
4. This has happened before ("last time"), and she remembers it — you don't.

## Clues planted in Part 1
* The photo was taken from **behind** you, inside the room → something is (or was) in 2B with you.
* Clock reads 3:41 in the photo stamp; the wall clock never moves.
* Your crush is confused when the voice says "you know I like you" — she didn't know.

## Part 1 choice
* **A — HIDE IN THE STORAGE ROOM.** Risk: only one way out; if it gets in, you're trapped.
* **B — ESCAPE THROUGH THE WINDOW.** Risk: 2nd floor; something is waiting in the courtyard.

## Outline (adjust to the comments)
2. **The Storage Room / The Courtyard** — (A) dark, cramped; she tells you part of what happened "last time"; the knocking moves to the storage door. (B) you drop to the ledge; the figure in the courtyard looks up — it has *your* face.
3. **Her Secret** — she admits this is not the first loop; she's tried to save you before; she disagrees with your plan.
4. **The Roll Call** — the intercom starts calling students who aren't in school any more.
5. **Your Shadow** — your shadow moves a beat late; she saves you.
6. **The Other Classroom** — Room 2B exists twice; the photo was taken from the other one.
7. **What It Remembers** — the thing wears your memories; romance peak — she chooses you over escaping.
8. **The Last Bell** — the final bell decides who leaves the building.

## Storytelling rules
* She has her own personality, fears and secrets; she sometimes disagrees with you and sometimes saves you.
* The romance grows a little every part (one small real moment per part, even mid-horror).
* The choice must visibly change the next part.
