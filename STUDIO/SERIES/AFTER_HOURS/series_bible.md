# AFTER HOURS — series bible

Horror series. Read before writing each part; update after it ships and when the top comment decides.

## Premise
Five friends (YOU, Kai, Ben, Mia, Zoe) game until 4 AM the night before the final exam and all fail. Kai's plan: sneak
into school at midnight and fix the answer sheets in Room 12. But the sheets are already graded in red — "SEE ME.
TONIGHT." — and the teacher who marks after hours takes attendance. Anyone not in a seat gets detention. Forever.

## Format (every part)
* 9:16, ~65 s, second-person present tense; all of `STUDIO/STANDARDS.md` applies (jumpscare + top-comment rules).
* Hook = flash-forward of the break-in, then "Rewind." to the relatable cause.
* Voices (Qwen3-TTS, `VIDEOS/VIDEO_013/voices/`): narrator · **MIA** · **INTERCOM** (the teacher, `fx: intercom`).
* The teacher: grey face, tight bun, round glasses that reflect white (you never see her eyes), thin wide smile. Seen
  only in jumpscares and as a silhouette.
* Choice screen: A/B pills + yellow **TOP COMMENT DECIDES PART N+1** banner; narrator reads the rule.

## Rules (hinted, never stated outright)
1. She only marks after hours; whoever she marks "SEE ME" has to see her.
2. Attendance: if you are in a seat, you are "present". If you are not, you are "absent" — and absent students vanish.
3. The board counts down.
4. Nobody remembers her class from the daytime. (Is Room 12 even used?)
