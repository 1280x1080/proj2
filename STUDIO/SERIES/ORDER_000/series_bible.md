# ORDER #000 — series bible

Horror series. Read before writing each part; update after it ships and when the top comment decides.

## Premise
2 AM. You're in bed watching mukbangs and order food on QuickBite. The driver knocks before you've put your phone
down — the restaurant is 40 minutes away. The app says your real driver (Sam) is still at the restaurant. The thing at
the door only *looks* like a delivery guy. He doesn't deliver food. The bag is how he gets in.

## Format (every part)
* 9:16, ~65 s, second-person present tense; all of `STUDIO/STANDARDS.md` applies (incl. jumpscare + top-comment rules).
* Hook = a relatable late-night moment with one wrong number in it ("driver 1 min away / restaurant 40 min away").
* Jumpscares: max 2 per part, music drops out first, never during a spoken line.
* Voices (Qwen3-TTS, `VIDEOS/VIDEO_012/voices/`): narrator · **DELIVERY GUY** (flat, too polite; `fx: door`) ·
  **MUKBANG GIRL** (`fx: phone`). Labels: `DELIVERY GUY · AT THE DOOR`, `MUKBANG GIRL · ON YOUR PHONE`.
* Apps on screen are generic originals (QuickBite, a vertical-video feed) — never a real app's UI.
* Choice screen: A/B pills + the big yellow **TOP COMMENT DECIDES PART N+1** banner; the narrator reads the rule.

3. People who survived him can warn others — but only through a screen.
