# FLIGHT 417 — series bible

Horror series. Read before writing each part; update after it ships and when the comments decide.

## Premise
Ten years ago Flight 417 took off just before midnight and vanished over the ocean — no wreckage, no signal. Tonight
**you** are in seat 14A on the same route. At 2:17 AM the lights flicker, the ground goes silent, and Flight 417 is
flying right beside you, full of passengers who have not aged. It has room for everyone.

## Format (every part)
* 9:16, ~80–90 s, second-person present-tense narration; all of `STUDIO/STANDARDS.md` applies.
* Hook = one "Imagine…" line over a radar blip dying + a flash-forward of the ghost plane in your window.
* Voices (Qwen3-TTS, in `VIDEOS/VIDEO_010/voices/`): narrator (low calm baritone) · **OLD MAN** (gentle, hoarse) ·
  **CAPTAIN** (PA, `fx: intercom`). Labels: `OLD MAN`, `CAPTAIN`.
* Look: normal night cabin → cold emergency lighting after the flicker (the room darkens, faces stay lit). The dead of
  Flight 417 are grey faces with hollow eyes behind glass — never seen closer than a window.
* Choice screen: "WHAT DO YOU DO?" + A/B pills with the risk under each; top comment decides.

## Characters

| **OLD MAN** (14B; grey hair, glasses, brown cardigan) | his wife was on Flight 417; he flies the route every year; his watch is **stopped at 2:17**; he unbuckled and wants to go to her — "Come with us" |
| **HIS WIFE** (Flight 417, window seat) | hasn't aged a day; she did not wave — she **pointed behind you** (a warning?) |
| **CAPTAIN** | first: "We've lost contact with the ground." Then, same voice: "This is Flight 417. We have room for everyone." |

## Rules (hinted, never stated outright)
1. It happens at 2:17 AM, every year, on this route. The old man knew.
2. People don't get taken by force: seats empty one by one with the belts still buckled — they *go*.
3. The exit only unlocks for someone who is wanted on the other side.
4. The wife pointed behind you: she is trying to warn, not to invite.

## Clues planted in Part 1
* The watch stopped at 2:17 before the lights flickered.
* The belts of the vanished passengers are still fastened.
* "Come with **us**", not "come with me".

## Part 1 choice
* **A — HOLD HIM BACK.** Risk: the door is opening for you too.
* **B — RUN TO THE COCKPIT.** Risk: who is flying this plane?

## Outline (adjust to the comments)

5. 2:17 never ends — the watch, the screens, your phone.
