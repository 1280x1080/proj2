# LAST ONE IN THE CALL — series bible

Horror series. Read before writing each part; update after it ships and when the comments decide.

## Premise
A normal night: you and the boys (Jay, Marcus, Dee) are gaming in voice chat. They leave one by one without saying
goodbye. Something is going from house to house wearing the face of the last friend it visited — and the voice chat
is how it finds the next one.

## Format (every part)
* 9:16, ~60 s, second-person present tense; all of `STUDIO/STANDARDS.md` applies.
* Hook = a relatable gaming moment turned wrong ("your whole squad left the voice chat — so who is still breathing in
  your headset?"), shown on the call UI.
* **Jumpscares (max 2 per part):** music drops to near silence first, then a hard cut to the grinning face with a
  slam zoom, shake, white flash and `jumpscare_sting`; 0.7 s each, never during a spoken line.
* Voices (Qwen3-TTS, `VIDEOS/VIDEO_011/voices/`): narrator (low calm baritone) · **JAY** (teen gamer, `fx: phone`).
  Speaker label: `JAY · VOICE CHAT`.
* The game on screen is always a generic original HUD (never a real game's art or characters).
* Choice screen: "WHAT DO YOU DO?" + A/B pills with the risk under each; top comment decides.

## Characters
| who | notes after Part 1 |
|---|---|
| **YOU** (mascot, red cap, green headset) | alone in your room at 1 AM; door now open behind you; something at your window |
| **JAY** | lives on the 3rd floor; saw "Marcus" smiling outside his window; screamed; his mic is still open and something breathes on it; it said "Your turn. Look behind you." |
| **MARCUS** | left the call first; texted "don't answer jay / that's not him anymore" — so is the real Marcus alive? |
| **DEE** | left second, no goodbye; not heard from |

## Rules (hinted, never stated outright)
1. It knocks first. Always three knocks, always on a window.
2. It wears the face of the last person it took (Marcus's face at Jay's window → Jay's voice in the call).
3. It can only come in after you look at it / answer it.
4. The call is its map: whoever is still connected is next.

## Part 1 choice

* **B — KEEP YOUR EYES ON THE SCREEN.** Risk: it's already walking toward you.
